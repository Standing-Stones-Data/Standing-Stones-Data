##  About Me 
I’m a data-focused person with a strong passion for Power BI and a drive to make information easier to understand and more useful. With 9 years of experience in Power BI, 8 years in SQL, and several years working with Power Apps and Power Automate, I’ve built end-to-end solutions that bring data to life—from fleet usage apps to call centre platforms. I enjoy bridging the gap between raw data and real-world decisions, and I find it especially rewarding when others start to see the value and potential in the data they already have.

When I'm not working with data, I enjoy juggling, double bass playing and Hiking.I’m driven by those moments when data brings new insights to light.
